I am a DPhil student in Rachael Bashford-Rogers' group at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), UK, working in collaboration with Daniel Dimitrov at [EMBL](https://www.embl.org/), Heidelberg, Germany. My research focuses on developing statistical and computational methods for spatial and single-cell genomics, in particular to understand how cells communicate within tissues in health and disease.

I am also a scientific researcher at the Applied Genomics Institute, King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST), Saudi Arabia.

## Research interests

- Spatial transcriptomics and single-cell multi-omics
- Cell–cell communication inference
- Statistical and computational methods for biological systems
- B cell biology and immunology
