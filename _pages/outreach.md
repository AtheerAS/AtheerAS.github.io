Alongside my research, I am co-founder and CEO of [Dalah](https://www.dalah.org), a non-profit initiative in Saudi Arabia that equips people with the skills to work with biological data, from statistical methods and tool building to high-performance computing, best practices and FAIR data principles.

Dalah is officially registered with the National Center for Non-Profit Sector (NCNP) in Saudi Arabia, operates under the supervision of the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), and is recognised by the [Misk Initiatives Incubator](https://hub.misk.org.sa/programs/community/misk-initiatives-incubator/).

To learn more about Dalah, visit [www.dalah.org](https://www.dalah.org).
